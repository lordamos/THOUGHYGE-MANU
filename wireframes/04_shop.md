## Key Features & Interactions

### E-commerce Functionality
- Shopping cart with quantity management
- Multiple product formats (digital, physical)
- Secure checkout process
- Product reviews and ratings
- Wishlist functionality (future)

### Product Display
- High-quality product images with zoom
- Multiple product variants (formats, sizes)
- Clear pricing and availability
- Product recommendations and upsells

### Navigation & Filtering
- Category-based browsing
- Search functionality with filters
- Sort options (price, popularity, rating)
- Breadcrumb navigation

### Trust & Security
- Customer reviews and testimonials
- Secure checkout badges
- Clear shipping and return policies
- SSL security indicators

## Responsive Behavior

### Desktop (1200px+)
- Four-column product grid
- Detailed product information
- Sidebar cart functionality

### Tablet (768px - 1199px)
- Two-column product grid
- Condensed product cards
- Overlay cart modal

### Mobile (< 768px)
- Single-column product listing
- Stack layout for product details
- Full-screen cart experience
- Touch-optimized buttons and forms

## Payment Integration

### Checkout Process
1. Cart review and item management
2. Shipping information (physical products)
3. Payment method selection
4. Order review and confirmation
5. Receipt and order tracking

### Payment Options
- Credit/debit cards (Stripe integration)
- PayPal
- Apple Pay / Google Pay (mobile)
- Buy now, pay later options (future)

### Digital Product Delivery
- Instant download links after purchase
- Email delivery with access instructions
- User account download history (future)
- DRM-free files where applicable

## Inventory Management

### Stock Tracking
- Real-time inventory levels
- Low stock warnings
- Out-of-stock notifications
- Backorder management

### Product Types
- **Physical Products**: Books, tools, jewelry
- **Digital Products**: PDFs, audio files, guides
- **Bundles**: Course + book packages
- **Pre-orders**: Upcoming releases

## Customer Support

### Help Features
- Product Q&A section
- Live chat support (future)
- Email support contact
- FAQ section with common questions
- Size guides and product specifications

### Order Management
- Order tracking and status updates
- Easy returns and exchanges
- Customer order history
- Reorder functionality
