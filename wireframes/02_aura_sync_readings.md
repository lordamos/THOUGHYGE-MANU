## Key Features & Interactions

### Hero Section
- Geometric pattern background with sacred geometry visuals
- Clear value proposition and main CTA
- Builds anticipation for the reading experience

### Service Description
- Visual explanation with mandala/geometric imagery
- Clear benefits and what clients can expect
- Builds trust and understanding

### What You'll Receive
- Six-card grid showing all deliverables
- Comprehensive overview of value
- Addresses client expectations

### Pricing Tiers
- Three clear options with "Most Popular" highlight
- Feature comparison for easy decision-making
- Clear pricing and duration

### Booking Form
- Comprehensive yet user-friendly form
- Calendar widget for date/time selection
- Personal information collection for preparation
- Terms and marketing opt-in checkboxes

### Process Explanation
- Three-step visual process
- Reduces anxiety about booking
- Sets clear expectations

### Social Proof
- Client testimonials with star ratings
- Real names for authenticity
- Link to additional reviews

### FAQ Section
- Collapsible/expandable questions
- Addresses common concerns
- Reduces booking friction

## Responsive Behavior

### Desktop (1200px+)
- Full three-column pricing layout
- Side-by-side content sections
- Large booking form with calendar widget

### Tablet (768px - 1199px)
- Two-column layouts for pricing
- Stacked content sections
- Optimized form layout

### Mobile (< 768px)
- Single-column pricing cards
- Stacked sections throughout
- Touch-optimized form inputs
- Condensed calendar widget

## Form Validation & UX

### Required Fields
- Full Name, Email, Phone, Time Zone
- Date/Time selection
- Terms agreement checkbox

### Error States
- Real-time validation feedback
- Clear error messages
- Highlight required fields

### Success States
- Confirmation message after submission
- Email confirmation sent to user
- Clear next steps communicated

## Integration Points

### Backend (Netlify Functions)
- Form submission processing
- Email notifications to admin
- Calendar availability checking
- Data storage for booking management

### Calendar System
- Available time slot display
- Time zone conversion
- Booking conflict prevention

### Email Automation
- Confirmation emails to clients
- Preparation instructions
- Reminder emails
